MY JOURNEY

My Engineering Journey

From marine engineering fundamentals to computational research and real-world offshore engineering.

2021–2025

Building the Foundation

IIT Kharagpur

Dual Degree — B.Tech + M.Tech in Ocean Engineering & Naval Architecture.

Built a strong foundation in hydrostatics, stability, marine hydrodynamics, offshore structures, ship resistance, propulsion, ocean engineering and computational methods.

CGPA: 8.81 / 10 Institute Silver Medal
2024

First Step into Engineering Applications

Naval Architect Intern — IIT Kharagpur

Applied naval architecture concepts to realistic vessel engineering problems involving dynamic positioning, thruster failure scenarios, vessel stability and resistance estimation.

Developed a C# algorithm for vessel stability under upright and damage conditions and estimated resistance and power using the Holtrop method.

Key Learning Understanding how marine engineering principles translate into practical engineering decisions.
2024

Entering Offshore Analysis

Installation & Analysis Engineering Intern — TechnipFMC

Gained practical exposure to offshore engineering through static and dynamic OrcaFlex analysis of marine risers across multiple loading conditions.

Generated 96 simulation files and developed Python-based automation for high-volume post-processing workflows.

Key Learning Dynamic offshore analysis, engineering workflows and analysis automation.
2024–2025

Researching Complex Marine Behaviour

M.Tech Research — IIT Kharagpur

Investigated vortex-induced vibration (VIV) of flexible marine risers under the Deep Ocean Mission project.

Developed a fluid-structure interaction framework using finite-element methods within an Arbitrary Lagrangian- Eulerian (ALE) formulation.

The research involved CFD, FSI, flexible riser modelling, large-scale computational simulations and supercomputer- based analysis involving 4.2M+ nodes.

Result Up to 40% reduction in VIV amplitude.
2025–Present

From Research to Real Engineering

Naval Architect — Research & Development

At QuantSolar Technologies, my work moved from academic and research problems to real engineering projects involving floating solar systems.

Hydrodynamics & Floating Systems

CFD simulations using ANSYS Fluent, hydrostatic analysis using Maxsurf, and hydrodynamic assessments using ANSYS AQWA.

Mooring & Anchoring

OrcaFlex-based station-keeping analysis considering wind, wave and current loading, including shore and underwater anchoring systems, intact and damage cases, and site-specific configurations.

Research & Development

Investigating elastic mooring concepts for floating solar platforms and contributing to the technical evaluation of new floating solar designs.

Computational Engineering & Automation

Developing engineering workflows using Python, VBA, AutoLISP, OrcaFlex Python API and OrcaWave/OrcaFlex data extraction.

Technical Coordination & External Collaboration

Coordinating technical reviews with IIT faculty, RINA, IRS and DNV, while collaborating with IIT Madras on experimental studies for new floating solar designs.

Key Learning Engineering goes beyond simulation — it involves technical communication, independent review, design iteration, validation and collaboration.
